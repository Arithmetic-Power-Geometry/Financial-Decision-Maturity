# Experiment plan

The synthetic benchmark is only Stage 1.

## Stage 1 — Controlled consistency
- known latent financial risk;
- sequentially improving evidence;
- asymmetric false-negative and false-positive costs;
- delayed evidence;
- costly secondary review;
- baseline comparison.

## Stage 2 — Stress tests
- prevalence shift;
- concept drift;
- label delay;
- calibration perturbation;
- review-quality variation;
- delay-cost variation;
- cost-ratio variation;
- evidence-order permutation.

## Stage 3 — Real public finance data
The framework should be tested on public, legally usable datasets with
time/order information where possible. The manuscript must clearly state
which evidence is genuinely sequential versus synthetically staged.

## Required reporting
- expected realized cost;
- decision accuracy;
- adverse-event recall;
- mean decision time;
- escalation rate;
- action-reversal rate;
- calibration;
- confidence intervals across random seeds.

Do not claim deployment efficacy from synthetic experiments.
